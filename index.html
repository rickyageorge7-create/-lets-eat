GOOD QUESTION

Quick answers.

Where do you deliver?

We currently serve Brewerville, Monrovia Central, and nearby Monrovia neighborhoods. Choose your zone at checkout to check the delivery fee.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery, Orange Money, and MTN MoMo. Mobile money instructions will appear as soon as you select a method at checkout.

Can I pick up my order?

Absolutely. Choose Pickup at checkout and we’ll confirm your order and pickup details by WhatsApp.

How do I check on my order?

Enter the order number from your confirmation and the phone number used at checkout in the Track Order section below.
